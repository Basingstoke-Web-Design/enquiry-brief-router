Commercial cleaning company: enquiry form plan

Keep the first step short enough to complete on a phone.

The form’s job
- A quote

Suggested initial fields
- Name
- Preferred reply method, then the email address or phone number the customer chooses
- Service needed
- General area or postcode
- Approximate scope
- Short description of what help is needed

Ask later
- Photographs
- Detailed site information

After someone sends the form
- Responsible team: Office team
- Confirmed response arrangement: During the next working day, subject to opening hours.
- Show a clear confirmation explaining what happens next.
- Make required fields obvious, explain errors and test successful delivery to the team.
- Provide a suitable privacy notice and decide what the business will retain.
